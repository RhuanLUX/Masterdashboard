`golden-source.png` is preserved unmodified. `fly-0.png`, `fly-1.png`, `fly-2.png` are the three 144×128 cells of the east-facing flight row, extracted by `tools/prepare-dragon-flight.cjs`. Only cropping was performed. The CSS/JavaScript presentation adds flight paths, timing, mirroring and atmospheric opacity; no source artwork is redrawn. All files load locally.
